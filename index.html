Why Certification Matters

Not all "Android TV boxes" are created equal. This guide focuses exclusively on Google/Netflix certified devices with Widevine L1 DRM that support native Netflix at 1080p or 4K and native Amazon Prime Video.

We exclude cheap uncertified boxes that only run Netflix at SD/720p or rely on sideloaded APKs for streaming services.
